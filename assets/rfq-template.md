# RFQ draft template

Copy this file, fill every field, and send the identical text to each candidate. All placeholders below are **synthetic examples of field names**, not supplier data. Delete any section that does not apply and say why.

Keep Chinese lines verbatim when writing to a supplier in China; the English line is a gloss for the buyer.

## 3. Supplier role / 供应商身份

```
请问贵公司是生产厂家还是贸易公司？
Are you the manufacturer or a trading company?

生产主体的公司全称和注册地址是什么？
What is the full legal name and registered address of the producing entity?

哪些工序自有完成，哪些外协？
Which processes are in-house and which are subcontracted?

模具/工装归谁所有？
Who owns the tooling and moulds?

能否提供营业执照与经营范围？
Can you provide the business licence and registered business scope?
```

## 4. Quotation request / 报价要求

Please quote / 请报价:

- Unit price at each quantity break / 各数量档单价
- Tooling or mould cost, separately / 模具费（单列）
- Sample charge and sample lead time / 样品费与打样周期
- Packaging and artwork cost / 包装与印刷费用
- MOQ / 起订量
- Production lead time / 生产交期
- Delivery term with named place / 贸易术语及指定地点 (e.g. FOB {{named_port}})
- Payment terms / 付款方式
- Quote validity / 报价有效期
- Currency and tax treatment / 币种与含税/不含税

## 5. Verification request / 核验资料

```
请提供：营业执照、设备清单、相关证书（如有）、质检报告样本。
Please provide: business licence, equipment list, relevant certificates (if any), and a sample QC report.

可否约定时间视频看车间、原料区与质检区？
Can we schedule a live video walk of the workshop, raw material area and QC station?

是否接受样品单与出货前验货？
Do you accept a sample order and pre-shipment inspection?
```

## 6. Closing / 结尾

```
请先确认规格与身份信息，再提供报价。所有资料仅用于本次评估。
Please confirm the specification and entity details before quoting. All materials will be used only for this evaluation.
```

---

**Before sending:** confirm all dimensions, materials, grades, tolerances and MOQ directly with the supplier; do not rely on machine translation for specifications. Note that any document received is a supplier claim until independently verified. This template is not legal advice.
